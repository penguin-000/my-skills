# Meeting notes

Work from the transcript or notes supplied by the user. Summarize the meeting purpose, key discussion points and confirmed decisions. List action items with owner and due date only when explicitly provided; mark missing owners or dates as unassigned. Separate unresolved questions from decisions. Do not invent attendance, approvals or commitments. Remove repetition while preserving disagreement that affects next steps. Do not send the minutes to anyone unless the user explicitly requests it.
